feat(c4): Structurizr-style visuals for the unified C4 renderer (opt-in via `c4.useUnifiedRenderer`). Renders elements in the c4model.com outline style (light fill, colored border and matching colored text, no shadow), with a muted `[Stereotype: Technology]` typography layout, dashed relationships with clean labels, lighter dashed boundaries, and a richer shape vocabulary (folder, object-storage bucket, terminal/server-side app, browser/single-page app) selectable through the `$shape` and `$sprite` attributes.
