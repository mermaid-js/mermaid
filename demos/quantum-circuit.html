    M[q0]

Quantum Teleportation

quantumCircuit-beta LR
    wires msg(psi), alice(0), bob(0)
    cbits c_msg, c_alice
    H[alice]
    CNOT[alice, bob]
    CNOT[msg, alice]
    H[msg]
    M[msg]   -> c_msg
    M[alice] -> c_alice
    X[bob] if c_alice
    Z[bob] if c_msg

Toffoli with Zero-Control

quantumCircuit-beta LR
    wires a(0), b(0), c(0)
    H[a]
    H[b]
    CCX[!a, b, c]
    M[a]
    M[b]
    M[c]

    CZ[q1, oracle]

    H[q0]

    H[q2]
    H[oracle]
    X[q0]
    X[q1]
    X[q2]
    X[oracle]
    CZ[q0, oracle]
    CZ[q1, oracle]
    CZ[q2, oracle]
    X[q0]
    X[q1]
    X[q2]
    X[oracle]
    H[q0]
    H[q1]
    H[q2]
    H[oracle]
    M[q0]
    M[q1]
    M[q2]
