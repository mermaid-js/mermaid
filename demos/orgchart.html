Org chart diagram demo

Browser and print-test gallery. Open this page in each browser and compare labels, reporting lines, staff placement, and chart boundaries. In print preview, check clipping, page breaks, contrast, and legibility. Wide or tall charts may require landscape orientation, larger paper, or print-dialog scaling; the gallery does not hide overflow or automatically paginate charts.

For a screen-reader check, use the accessibility example below. Confirm its title and relationship description are announced. The hierarchy is not keyboard-navigable; the text outline provides an alternative to following the visual connectors.

config:

Accessible title, description, and text alternative

orgchart-beta
title Company reporting structure
accTitle: Company organization
accDescr {
    Anna is the CEO. Engineering and Sales report to Anna.
    Bob in Finance provides staff support to Anna, not a reporting branch.
}
    CEO "Anna"
        staff Finance "Bob"
        Engineering
        Sales

Equivalent text outline:

  • CEO: Anna
    • Reporting units: Engineering and Sales.
    • Staff support: Finance (Bob).
